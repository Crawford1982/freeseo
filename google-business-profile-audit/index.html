Who this is for

This page is for UK businesses that rely on Google Maps, calls, directions and local search. Your Google Business Profile does not work in isolation. Google compares the profile with your website, contact details, categories, reviews, location wording and structured data. If those signals disagree or the website is thin, your profile has less support than it should.

What I would fix first

  • Match NAP details across site and profile
  • Add a clear contact/location block
  • Use LocalBusiness schema for the main entity
  • Link service pages from the homepage and Google profile where relevant

A GBP audit should answer one question: does the website make the profile more believable? If not, fixing the website can improve calls and directions without changing your whole brand.

How to judge success

Do not judge this only by a score. Judge it by whether more people can find you, understand what you do, and take action. After making fixes, watch Google Search Console impressions, Google Business Profile calls/directions, form submissions and quote requests for 30 days.
