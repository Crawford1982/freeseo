Frequently asked questions

What is a free my site audit?
A my site audit checks your website and local SEO basics: speed and mobile experience, title and meta tags, HTTPS, schema, and signals that support your Google Business Profile. MySiteAudit runs a free my site audit in your browser in about 30 seconds — enter your URL above, no signup required.
Why isn't my business showing on Google?
Usually it's one of: no Google Business Profile (or it's unclaimed), a slow or broken website, missing location keywords and NAP (name, address, phone) on your site, or no local schema markup. Run the free audit above to see exactly what's wrong for your site.
What is local SEO?
Local SEO is how you get found when people search for your type of business near them (e.g. "plumber Manchester"). It relies on your Google Business Profile, your website's speed and structure, and consistent name/address/phone info across the web.
Is this free local SEO audit really free?
Yes. No signup, no email required. You get an instant report. We never sell or share your details. If you want someone to fix the issues for you, you can get in touch — that's optional and up to you.
How do I get my business on Google Maps?
Claim and complete your Google Business Profile at business.google.com. Add your address, phone, opening hours, photos, and category. Keep it updated. Our audit checks whether your website supports your GBP (e.g. same NAP, schema) so you rank better.
