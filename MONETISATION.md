# How this site makes money (without selling user data)

- **Primary:** People who want help fixing their site **contact you** (email). You quote them (e.g. £499 website). You never sell or pass their details to anyone — they choose to reach out.
- **Optional affiliate:** In the "What to do next" section you can add affiliate links (e.g. hosting, SEO tools) and add one line: "Some links are affiliate; we get a small commission at no extra cost to you." Still no selling of user data.
- **Optional support:** Add a "Buy me a coffee" / Ko-fi link in the footer for voluntary tips. Keeps the tool free; some users will chip in.
- **Trust:** Privacy and "we never sell your data" are stated in the CTA and footer so users feel safe and more likely to contact you when they need help.

Update `canonical`, `og:url`, and `og:image` in `index.html` when you have your live domain and an OG image.
