About

Why MySiteAudit Exists

MySiteAudit is a free local SEO audit tool built for UK small businesses. It checks the common reasons a business doesn't appear on Google or Google Maps and explains exactly what to fix—no jargon, no fluff.

About the Author

MySiteAudit was created by James Crawford, who builds websites and tools for small businesses across the UK.

If your business isn't appearing on Google, or you'd like help fixing the issues the audit tool finds, you can reach James at help@mysiteaudit.co.uk.

How It Works

The audit tool runs in your browser and checks:

  • Website speed and mobile experience
  • SEO signals like titles, meta tags, and headings
  • Local visibility factors including NAP consistency and schema markup
  • Technical issues that affect Google's ability to crawl and rank your site

The report is instant and plain-English. No signup required. No data sold. It's completely free to use.
